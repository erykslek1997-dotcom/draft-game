Conclusions:
- **Needed:** the D-TAL bridge and the role resume. Without the resume the forecast collapses, 0.671 → 0.629.
- **Small value:** the low-usage lift.
- **Defense credit:** carries information (r 0.685 vs 0.678), but at full strength for non-scorers it overrates them. The gate keeps the information and removes the overrating.
- **No measurable effect on 1997+ data:** usage scale, position ceilings, named adjustments, reduced role, old-school PG, small-role cap. They act on older windows or on single players. There is nothing to cut on this evidence, and no data says they help either.
- **Offense specialists read +11 over RAPM.** RAPM is known to undervalue high-usage creators, and the user's principle is that offense rules in the modern game. Left alone.
